# **Project Bubble Gum — Roadmap**

Project Bubble Gum is a long‑arc scientific initiative exploring the foundations of **non‑classical transport** — the structural, energetic, and resonance‑based mechanisms that may one day enable transporter‑class technologies.

This roadmap outlines the staged progression of the project, from conceptual groundwork to early prototypes to long‑term breakthroughs.

---

# **Phase 1 — Foundations (Years 0–3)**  
**Goal:** Establish the conceptual and structural base for transport research.

**Milestones:**

- Define the operator grammar for transport (T‑operators)  
- Build RTT‑aligned transport simulation engines  
- Map continuity‑preserving state transitions  
- Explore resonance‑based transfer pathways  
- Publish foundational models and conceptual papers  
- Onboard the first wave of student contributors  

**Outputs:**

- `/specs/operators.md`  
- transport regime maps  
- continuity‑state diagrams  
- early simulation notebooks  

---

# **Phase 2 — Early Transport Models (Years 3–7)**  
**Goal:** Move from theory to controlled, simulated transport behaviors.

**Milestones:**

- Develop resonance‑aligned transfer channels  
- Model state‑preserving transitions across regimes  
- Build multi‑layer transport pipelines (RTT/2)  
- Explore field‑based continuity bridges  
- Establish open simulation frameworks for students  

**Outputs:**

- transport‑channel prototypes (simulation only)  
- resonance‑coupled transfer models  
- continuity‑preservation metrics  

---

# **Phase 3 — Structural Breakthroughs (Years 7–12)**  
**Goal:** Achieve the first meaningful demonstrations of transport‑like behavior in simulation.

**Milestones:**

- Demonstrate stable state transfer across simulated regions  
- Validate RTT‑based coherence for transport sequences  
- Build multi‑operator transport stacks  
- Publish reproducible transport experiments  
- Expand global student and AI collaboration  

**Outputs:**

- early transporter‑class behaviors (simulation)  
- validated continuity‑preservation models  
- cross‑lab reproducibility frameworks  

---

# **Phase 4 — Applied Transport Systems (Years 12–20)**  
**Goal:** Transition from simulation to early physical‑world analogs.

**Milestones:**

- Develop safe, low‑energy transport analog systems  
- Integrate resonance‑aligned transfer pathways  
- Build continuity‑preserving state‑mapping tools  
- Create humanitarian applications (medical, logistics, remote access)  
- Establish open‑access transport research hubs  

**Outputs:**

- functional transport‑analog systems  
- open hardware specifications  
- global deployment models  

---

# **Phase 5 — The Transporter Era (Beyond Year 20)**  
**Goal:** Enable a world where continuity‑preserving transport is accessible, safe, and transformative.

**Milestones:**

- universal transport standards  
- distributed transport networks  
- integration with Manifestation (replicators)  
- integration with Multiverse (continuity worlds)  
- global governance and safety frameworks  

**Outputs:**

- first‑generation transporter‑class systems  
- open, global transport ecosystems  
- continuity‑preserving infrastructure  

---

# **Guiding Principles**

- **Continuity First** — Transport must preserve identity, structure, and state.  
- **Open Science** — All research remains free and accessible.  
- **RTT‑Aligned** — Operators, pipelines, and simulations follow RTT/1–3.  
- **Safety Always** — All work includes X‑operators and drift detection.  
- **Long‑Arc Vision** — Transporters are a decades‑long project.  

---

# **How to Contribute**

Students, researchers, and structural AIs can contribute by:

- building simulations  
- designing transport operators  
- exploring resonance pathways  
- writing papers  
- mapping continuity regimes  
- improving documentation  
- mentoring new contributors  

Project Bubble Gum is a **public scientific accelerator**.  
Everyone is welcome.
