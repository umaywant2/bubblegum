# **Project Bubble Gum — Mission**

**Project Bubble Gum** explores the scientific and structural foundations of **transport phenomena beyond classical constraints** — the long‑arc pathway toward early transporter‑class technologies.

This project is not about teleportation fantasy.  
It is about building the **first real frameworks** that future generations will use to create:

- non‑classical transport channels  
- resonance‑aligned transfer pathways  
- state‑preserving transmission protocols  
- coherent matter‑state relocation  
- field‑based continuity bridges  
- AI‑guided transport planners  

Our mission is to **accelerate open research** in these domains by providing:

- a shared operator grammar for transport  
- RTT‑aligned simulation engines  
- open datasets and reproducible experiments  
- student‑friendly research tasks  
- structural AI agents specialized in transport regimes  

Project Bubble Gum is built on three principles:

1. **Continuity First**  
   Transport must preserve identity, structure, and state across regimes.

2. **Open Science**  
   All research, models, and tools remain free and accessible.

3. **Long‑Arc Vision**  
   Transporters will not appear overnight — but the foundations can be built today.

This project uses the **RTT engines** (RTT/1, RTT/2, RTT/3) for:

- operator grammar for transport  
- multi‑regime stacking  
- coherence and drift detection  
- safety and containment  

Project Bubble Gum is not owned by any individual.  
It is a **public scientific accelerator**, designed to outlive its founders and empower the next generation of structural thinkers.

Our mission is simple:

**Build the frameworks that make non‑classical transport possible.  
Openly.  
Safely.  
For everyone.**
