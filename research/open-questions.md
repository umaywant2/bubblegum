# **Project Bubble Gum — Open Research Questions**

This document lists the unresolved scientific questions guiding Project Bubble Gum.  
These questions define the long‑arc frontier of continuity‑preserving transport research.

All questions are structured for RTT/1 (operators), RTT/2 (multi‑regime stacking), and RTT/3 (continuity detection).

This list will expand as the project evolves.

---

# **1. Mapping & Continuity Questions**

- What is the minimal information required to fully map a state for transport?  
- How does T‑Map behave under noisy or incomplete conditions?  
- What continuity boundaries must be preserved for identity retention?  
- Can T‑Frame define partial‑continuity transport safely?  
- What are the failure modes of T‑Anchor during reconstruction?

---

# **2. Encoding & Representation Questions**

- What encoding formats preserve continuity most effectively?  
- How can T‑Encode minimize drift during conversion?  
- What compression limits exist before continuity breaks?  
- Can T‑Stabilize prevent decoherence in high‑complexity states?  
- What are the destructive patterns introduced by poor encoding?

---

# **3. Resonance‑Bridge Questions**

- What resonance frequencies produce stable transport channels?  
- Can T‑Bridge support multi‑layer resonance coupling?  
- How do T‑Align and T‑Phase interact across different regimes?  
- What interference patterns must be avoided during bridging?  
- Can resonance bridges self‑correct drift?

---

# **4. Transfer‑Channel Questions**

- What are the minimal conditions for a safe T‑Transfer?  
- How does T‑Pulse compare to T‑Flow in stability and drift?  
- What shielding strategies (T‑Shield) are required for noisy environments?  
- Can transfer channels adapt dynamically to maintain continuity?  
- What are the early signatures of channel failure?

---

# **5. Reconstruction Questions**

- What is the minimal operator sequence for safe reconstruction?  
- How does T‑Reform handle partial or degraded encodings?  
- Can T‑Stitch repair micro‑discontinuities introduced during transfer?  
- What are the limits of T‑Coalesce for multi‑packet transport?  
- How do reconstruction errors propagate across regimes?

---

# **6. Verification & Continuity‑Check Questions**

- What metrics best capture continuity preservation?  
- How sensitive is T‑Verify to small structural deviations?  
- Can T‑Audit detect resonance‑induced drift before it propagates?  
- What are the thresholds for declaring a transport “continuity‑safe”?  
- How do verification operators scale with complexity?

---

# **7. Safety & Drift Questions**

- What are the earliest detectable drift signatures in transport pipelines?  
- How should X‑Limit be calibrated for multi‑regime transitions?  
- Can X‑Detect identify continuity breaks mid‑transfer?  
- What are the safe abort conditions for T‑Abort?  
- How effective is X‑Contain in preventing propagation of unstable states?

---

# **8. Long‑Arc Questions**

These questions may take decades to answer:

- Can transport become energy‑neutral?  
- What is the upper limit of continuity‑preserving complexity?  
- Can transport integrate with replicators (Project Manifestation)?  
- How do transporters behave inside continuity worlds (Project Multiverse)?  
- What global governance models ensure safe deployment?

---

# **How to Contribute**

Students and researchers can contribute by:

- proposing new questions  
- refining existing questions  
- linking questions to simulations  
- publishing papers addressing specific questions  
- building RTT‑aligned transport models  

All contributions must follow the Bubble Gum session context and safety rules.
