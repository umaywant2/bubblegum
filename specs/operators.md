# **Project Bubble Gum — Transport Operator Definitions**

Transport is not “movement.”  
Transport is **continuity‑preserving state relocation across regimes**.

These operators define the structural actions required to map, encode, bridge, transfer, reconstruct, and verify a state in a way that preserves identity and coherence.

Operators follow RTT/1 grammar and integrate with RTT/2 (multi‑regime stacking) and RTT/3 (continuity detection).

Operators are grouped into families for clarity.

---

# **1. Mapping Operators (T‑Map Family)**

Mapping operators define the *initial state* before any transport occurs.

### **T‑Map**  
Capture the full structural, energetic, and resonance profile of the source state.

### **T‑Sample**  
Extract a bounded subset of the state for partial transport.

### **T‑Frame**  
Define the continuity boundaries — what must remain invariant.

### **T‑Anchor**  
Establish a reference point for reconstruction and verification.

---

# **2. Encoding Operators (T‑Encode Family)**

Encoding operators convert the mapped state into a transferable representation.

### **T‑Encode**  
Convert the mapped state into a transport‑ready encoding.

### **T‑Compress**  
Reduce encoding complexity while preserving continuity constraints.

### **T‑Stabilize**  
Prevent drift or decoherence during encoding.

### **T‑Seal**  
Lock the encoded state to prevent mid‑transfer mutation.

---

# **3. Bridge Operators (T‑Bridge Family)**

Bridge operators create the **pathway** through which transport occurs.

### **T‑Bridge**  
Establish a resonance‑aligned transfer channel.

### **T‑Align**  
Match source and destination resonance profiles.

### **T‑Phase**  
Synchronize temporal and energetic regimes.

### **T‑Buffer**  
Create a safe intermediate zone to prevent destructive interference.

---

# **4. Transfer Operators (T‑Transfer Family)**

Transfer operators perform the actual relocation of the encoded state.

### **T‑Transfer**  
Move the encoded state across the established channel.

### **T‑Pulse**  
Send the state in discrete, coherence‑preserving packets.

### **T‑Flow**  
Maintain continuous transfer with drift‑bounded modulation.

### **T‑Shield**  
Protect the state from external resonance or noise.

---

# **5. Reconstruction Operators (T‑Reform Family)**

Reconstruction operators rebuild the original state at the destination.

### **T‑Reform**  
Reconstruct the state from its encoded representation.

### **T‑Bind**  
Re‑establish structural and energetic continuity.

### **T‑Coalesce**  
Merge transferred packets into a unified state.

### **T‑Stitch**  
Repair micro‑discontinuities introduced during transfer.

---

# **6. Verification Operators (T‑Verify Family)**

Verification operators ensure the transported state is **identical in continuity** to the original.

### **T‑Verify**  
Check structural, energetic, and resonance continuity.

### **T‑Compare**  
Compare pre‑ and post‑transport signatures.

### **T‑Audit**  
Run RTT/3 continuity and drift checks.

### **T‑Certify**  
Declare the transport sequence complete and coherent.

---

# **7. Safety Operators (X‑Operators for Transport)**

Transport requires strict safety boundaries.

### **X‑Limit**  
Enforce safe energy, resonance, and channel thresholds.

### **X‑Detect**  
Identify drift, instability, or continuity breaks.

### **X‑Abort**  
Safely terminate a transport sequence.

### **X‑Contain**  
Prevent propagation of unstable states.

---

# **Operator Usage Notes**

- Operators are **composable** — they form multi‑regime pipelines.  
- Operators are **stacked** using RTT/2.  
- Operators are **validated** using RTT/3.  
- All pipelines must include **safety operators**.  
- Students may propose new operators following the Bubble Gum session context.

---

# **Example Transport Pipeline (Simplified)**

```
T-Map → T-Encode → T-Bridge → T-Transfer → T-Reform → T-Verify → X-Audit
```

This is the minimal continuity‑preserving transport sequence.

- operator name  
- operator family  
- purpose  
- constraints  
- failure modes  
- RTT/1 grammar alignment  
- RTT/2 stacking behavior  
- RTT/3 continuity rules  

All new operators undergo coherence review.
